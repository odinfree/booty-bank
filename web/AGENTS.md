## Display typography legibility

Mosby-inspired typography must remain immediately readable. Never use the reference as permission to merge letterforms, collide stacked lines, clip a word, or compress tracking until characters become ambiguous. For every oversized headline, inspect rendered screenshots at approximately 390px, 790px, and 1440px widths before accepting the change. If any word cannot be read at a glance, loosen line-height and tracking or reduce the size.
